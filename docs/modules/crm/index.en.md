# CRM { #crm }

Owners and employees use the CRM to set up the store and process orders. On your first visit, start with [connection and first sign-in](../../getting-started/registration.md), then follow the [store launch checklist](getting-started.md).

<div class="grid cards" markdown="1">

- :material-clipboard-list-outline: **Process an order**

    Check payment, allocate products, ship, and complete the order.

    [Orders](orders.md)

- :material-account-group-outline: **Find a customer**

    Customer details, purchase history, and bonus account.

    [Clients](clients.md)

- :material-package-variant-closed: **Prepare the catalog**

    Stores, categories, products, stock, and design.

    [Catalog](catalog.md)

- :material-ticket-percent-outline: **Prepare an offer**

    Promotions, promo codes, bonus points, and customer messages.

    [Marketing](marketing.md)

- :material-chart-line: **Review results**

    Sales, repeat purchases, and customer sources.

    [Dashboard and analytics](analytics.md)

- :material-tune-variant: **Change settings**

    App behavior, support contacts, and connected services.

    [General settings](general-settings.md) · [Integrations](integrations.md)

</div>

If you cannot complete a task, find your case in [Help](../../troubleshooting.md#store-owners). Customer actions are covered in the [customer app](../app/index.md) section.
