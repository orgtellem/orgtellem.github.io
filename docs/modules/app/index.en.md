# Customer app { #customer-app }

Open your store's link in a browser. If the store has connected a Telegram bot, you can also open the app from that bot. You can browse without signing in; to place an order, [sign in to your account](account.md#sign-in-and-registration).

<div class="grid cards" markdown="1">

- :material-cart-outline: **Choose products**

    Find a product, choose a variant, and check your cart.

    [Storefront and cart](ui-and-pages.md)

- :material-clipboard-check-outline: **Place an order**

    Choose fulfillment and payment, apply a promo code, and check order status.

    [Checkout and orders](checkout.md)

- :material-account-circle-outline: **Manage your account**

    Sign in, change personal details, connect bonus points, or install the app.

    [Account and installation](account.md)

- :material-bell-outline: **Receive notifications**

    Connect Telegram, VK, email, or notifications on your device.

    [Customer notifications](customer-notifications.md)

</div>

If you cannot pay, apply a promo code, or enable notifications, open [customer help](../../troubleshooting.md#customers). For questions about a purchase, contact your store through **Profile → Support**, if the store has added its contacts.

!!! info "For store owners"
    Store setup and order processing are covered in [CRM](../crm/index.md). Follow the [launch checklist](../crm/getting-started.md) before opening your store.
